General Daily Tips

  • Hydrate Consistently: Drink at least 2.5 to 3 liters of water daily to maintain optimal cognitive function, joint lubrication, and cellular repair.
  • Morning Sun & Movement: Aim for 15 minutes of natural morning sunlight and light mobility exercises within an hour of waking up to set circadian rhythm.
  • Mindful Nutrition: Practice chewing thoroughly and eating without screen distractions to promote healthy gut enzyme production and natural satiety cues.
  • Restorative Sleep Hygiene: Maintain a consistent sleep schedule aiming for 7-9 hours of uninterrupted sleep in a dark, cool environment.
